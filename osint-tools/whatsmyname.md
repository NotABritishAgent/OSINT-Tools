### **What does WhatsMyName do?**&#x20;

WhatsMyName allows users to quickly determine whether a username appears across multiple online platforms.

It performs automated checks against a curated database of sites and returns possible matches, confirmed accounts, or unavailable results.

**The lowdown:** The tool is a quick and effective way to identify where a username may appear online, particularly useful in the early stages of an OSINT investigation. However, results should always be treated as potential matches rather than confirmed accounts.

### How to Use:

1. **Go to the** [**WhatsMyName website.**<br>](https://whatsmyname.app/)
2. **Enter the username into the search field. E.g. We typed in OSINT Industries.**

3. **Run the search and wait for the scan to complete.**<br>
4. **Review the list of websites where the username appears. Results present in icons on the left (highlighted in Green) as well as in a searchable table on the right.**<br>
5. **Manually verify accounts to confirm they belong to the target individual.**

### &#x20;Use in Reporting

WhatsMyName is particularly useful for username enumeration, identifying digital footprints, online identity correlation, and early-stage OSINT investigations.&#x20;

Investigators often use the results to pivot to other OSINT techniques, such as profile analysis, social graph mapping, or image analysis.

| **Capabilities**                              | **Limitations**                                                                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Searches hundreds of websites simultaneously. | Matching usernames do not guarantee the same individual. False positives may also occur. |
| Easy to use with minimal technical knowledge. | Some platforms block automated detection.                                                |
| Rapid username remuneration.                  | Deleted/private accounts may still appear in results.                                    |
| Frequently updated site database.             | Not all websites are included in the database.                                           |
| <p><br></p>                                   | Requires manual verification.                                                            |

### Summary

WhatsMyName is a fast and accessible OSINT tool for username discovery across multiple platforms. It’s particularly valuable during the initial stages of online investigations, helping analysts identify where a username may exist online and where further investigation should be focused.

However, results should always be manually validated, as usernames alone are not sufficient to confirm identity.

### Ethical Considerations:

* Ensure searches are conducted for legitimate investigative or research purposes.
* Avoid harassment, stalking, or misuse of discovered accounts.
* Respect privacy and data protection regulations.
* Verify findings before drawing conclusions about an individual as false positives may occur.<br>
