### **What does DeepL Translator do?**&#x20;

DeepL Translator converts text from one language into another using advanced artificial intelligence. It can translate short pieces of text, long passages, or entire documents.

It supports many major languages and is known for high-quality, context-aware translations, meaning the translated text often reads more naturally.

**The lowdown:** DeepL is widely considered one of the most accurate online translation tools available. However, like all automated translation tools, it can still make mistakes, especially with slang, cultural references, or very technical language, so should always be double-checked.

### How to Use:

**1. Go to the DeepL website and paste the text you want to translate into the left-hand text box.**

**2. Select the language you want to translate into. (DeepL can often automatically detect the original language.)**&#x20;

_In the below (very basic!) example, we typed ‘Hallo’ into the left-hand text box which automatically detected German and translated into British English as selected._

### Cookies:&#x20;

DeepL uses cookies to support essential website functions, remember user preferences (such as language settings), manage sessions, and collect analytics data.

### &#x20;Use in Reporting

DeepL can support investigations in several ways:

* Translating foreign-language social media posts.
* Understanding international news articles.
* Translating leaked documents or reports.
* Analysing online forums in different languages.
* Translating witness statements or interviews.

### Summary

DeepL Translator is a powerful and easy-to-use translation tool, often more natural and accurate than many other free tools. While it is very useful for analysing foreign-language content, translations should always be double-checked when accuracy is critical.



### Ownership

DeepL Translator is developed and operated by DeepL GmbH, a technology company based in Cologne, Germany. It was founded by Dr. Jaroslaw "Jarek" Kutyłowski, a computer scientist who was born in Poland and is now based in Germany.

### Ethical Considerations:

* Be careful when uploading sensitive or confidential information.
* Understand that automated translations may contain errors.
* Always verify important translations with a native speaker or additional sources if accuracy is critical.
* Use translated information responsibly and within legal and ethical boundaries.
